# SDDS baseline conversion audit

Scope: homepage, work index, product listing, case study, and inquiry form at 1280 × 720.

## Evidence

1. `baseline/01-home.png` — strong visual identity, but a broad agency promise and no immediate proof.
2. `baseline/02-work.png` — premium work introduction, but the portfolio evidence begins below the fold.
3. `baseline/03-work-list.png` — project names and links are clear, but screenshots, operating problems, scope, and engagement context are absent.
4. `baseline/04-case-study.png` — polished case-study structure, but metrics and infrastructure language exceed what the public product can verify.
5. `baseline/05-contact.png` — concise inquiry surface, but it does not qualify stage, timing, engagement shape, or investment range.

## Baseline judgment

The visual system communicates precision and craft. Conversion is weakened by generic positioning, limited proof, unsupported social proof, and an unclear bridge between browsing work and starting the right kind of engagement.

## Implemented direction

- Lead with the business decision and working-product evidence.
- Add inspectable product screenshots and grounded release facts.
- Organize services around buyer situations and explicit engagement models.
- Bound case-study claims to public evidence and label status, platform, engagement, and deliverables.
- Replace the placeholder testimonial with demonstrable product proof.
- Qualify inquiries without making the form feel like procurement paperwork.
